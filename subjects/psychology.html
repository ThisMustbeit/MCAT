Sleep spindles and K complexes

Sleep spindles and K complexes are markers of stage 2 non-REM sleep.

  • Stage 2 non-REM sleep is the key association to remember.
  • Sleep spindles are short bursts of rhythmic brain-wave activity on EEG.
  • K complexes are large, high-amplitude waveforms that can appear spontaneously or after a stimulus.
  • Do not confuse these with REM sleep.
  • Do not confuse them with delta waves, which are more associated with deep stage 3 non-REM sleep.
  • This topic is more high-yield for psychology and biology than for chemistry.
